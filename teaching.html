My teaching approach

Build intuition

Begin with a clear question and a concrete example, then connect the intuition to mathematical definitions and results.

Explain the reasoning

Work through the key steps of a solution, helping students understand why a method applies and how to interpret the answer.

Connect theory and practice

Use R, Python and simulation to explore statistical ideas and connect theoretical results with observable behaviour.
