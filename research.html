Research / Dr Ariane Chrisko

Modelling dependence. Understanding uncertainty.

My work studies how uncertainty evolves over time and spreads across locations and networks.

Spatiotemporal volatility

My doctoral research develops an exponential spatiotemporal GARCH model with asymmetric volatility spillovers. It examines spatial dependence, dynamic volatility and the different effects of positive and negative shocks.

Financial networks and forecasting

I compare spatiotemporal and conventional multivariate volatility models, investigating how network specification affects forecast accuracy and computational cost.

Statistical computation

My methodological work uses likelihood-based estimation, numerical optimisation, Monte Carlo simulation and uncertainty quantification. I implement statistical methods in R and Python.
